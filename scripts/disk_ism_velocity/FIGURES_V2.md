# Publication figures v2 — descriptions

Generated by `scripts/disk_ism_velocity/make_paper_figures_v2.py` from the multi-ion
absorbing-gas catalog and the **supervisor-model** disk-ISM velocity
(`vism_tables_v2/vism_master_v2.csv`; v_ISM = v_rot(R_anchor)·proj, 3-tracer average with
cold gas T<10⁴). Style: Computer-Modern serif, inward ticks, framed legends, pdf+png.
Titles are intentionally omitted (publication style).

## Ions and spectral lines
Only ions that actually appear in the COS-G130M mock spectra (1150–1450 Å) are shown; the
gas-only ions O VI, C IV, Mg II and Fe II are **excluded** (they have no spectrum). Each ion
is labelled by its transition wavelength:

| Ion | Line(s) in the spectra (Å) | Label used |
|-----|----------------------------|------------|
| H I   | 1215.67 | H I λ1216 |
| O I   | 1302.17 | O I λ1302 |
| C II  | 1334.53 | C II λ1335 |
| Si II | **1190.42, 1193.29, 1260.42** (triplet) | Si II λ1260 (strongest; column is the Si⁺ ion column, common to all three lines) |
| Si III| 1206.50 | Si III λ1206 |
| Si IV | 1402.77 | Si IV λ1403 |
| N V   | 1238.82 | N V λ1239 |

Ions are ordered neutral → high ionization (H I, O I, C II, Si II, Si III, Si IV, N V) and
coloured cool→warm (turbo). Each distribution is weighted by **that ion's own column**.
Δv = v_los − v_ISM; kinematic class by |Δv|: **ISM <40, IVC 40–100, HVC >100 km s⁻¹**.

> Note on Si II: the spectra contain three Si II lines (1190, 1193, 1260), but they are
> transitions of the *same* Si⁺ ion and trace one column density — so there is a single Si II
> curve, labelled by the strongest line (1260 Å, the one used for the dip/Voigt analysis).

## The figures
1. **fig1_abundance_multiion** — (a) total ion column per sightline vs Δv, per ion; (b) the
   peak-normalised column-weighted PDF. The velocity distribution broadens with ionization —
   cool ions (H I, O I, C II, Si II) peak at the ISM, N V/Si IV reach into the HVC wings.
2. **fig2_hvc_multiion** — high-velocity gas (|Δv| ≥ 100): (a) per-ion abundance with the
   x-axis starting at 100 km s⁻¹; (b) the fraction of each ion's column in the IVC and HVC bins.
3. **fig3_inflow_outflow_multiion** — (a) inflowing fraction of the HVC column per ion
   (v_r < 0); (b) column-weighted PDFs of the galactocentric radial velocity v_r, showing the
   cool→warm inflow→outflow gradient.
4. **fig4_metallicity_multiion** — (a) Z/Z⊙ PDF per ion; (b) median HVC metallicity split into
   inflow vs outflow.
5. **fig5_ionization_temperature** — (a) gas-temperature PDF per ion; (b) median log T of the
   gas each ion traces, in the ISM vs the HVC — a clean ionization/temperature sequence.
6. **fig6_column_budget_multiion** — the fraction of each ion's total column residing in the
   ISM / IVC / HVC velocity bins (stacked), quantifying the phase where each ion's HVCs live.
7. **fig7_ionratio_vs_dv** — ionization-state proxies vs Δv: (a) N_SiIV/N_SiII, (b)
   N_SiIII/N_SiII. Both rise in the high-velocity wings (more ionized gas at large |Δv|).
   (In-band silicon ratios only — the earlier C IV/Si II panel is dropped, C IV has no spectrum.)
8. **fig8_detection_rate** — (a) HVC covering fraction per ion (fraction of sightlines with
   HVC absorption above the detection floor); (b) the per-galaxy HVC detection rate.
9. **fig9_hvc_phase_space** — where the HVCs are: (a) mean radial velocity ⟨v_r⟩ mapped in the
   (R, z) disk-frame plane; (b) v_r vs galactocentric radius r.

## Caveat
v_ISM here is the supervisor rotation-curve model, which is unbiased but coarse (≈ one value
per galaxy) and marks the major ISM absorption ~56% of the time overall (well for in-disk,
poorly beyond the disk edge). See `VISM_V2.md` and `diagnostics_v2/` for the full assessment.
