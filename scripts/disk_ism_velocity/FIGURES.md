## Sample
- **1,128,898** Si II-bearing ray cells over the 14,400 sightlines (20 SIDs × 360 α × 2 modes).
- Cells clustered into velocity components (absorbers): split where the velocity gap > 25 km/s,
  component Si II column threshold N_SiII > 10^12.5 cm^-2.
- **18,646 absorbers over 13,636 sightlines**; kinematic class by |Δv| = |v_los − v_ISM|:
  **ISM (<40) 51.3%, IVC (40–100) 20.1%, HVC (>100) 28.6%.**

- **fig5_hvc_phase_space** — where HVCs are. (a) ⟨v_r⟩ map in (R_disk, z_disk); (b) v_r vs

- **fig7_column_temperature_vs_dv** — Si II column and temperature vs Δv. Highest columns at the
  ISM velocity; two temperature populations (cool ISM at logT≈4.0, warmer HVCs at logT≈4.4–4.9).

## Key science
1. Absorber abundance peaks sharply at the disk-ISM velocity and falls ~exponentially into the
   HVC wings; **~29% of Si II absorbers are HVCs** but they carry only **6% of the Si II column**.
2. **Si II HVCs are predominantly inflowing (~64%) and metal-enriched (~solar)** — consistent with
   galactic-fountain recycling rather than pristine cold accretion.
3. The Δv < 100 km/s regime is deliberately excluded in fig2 because cloud association is ambiguous
   there (ISM/IVC blending).
