# Paper figures — multi-ion disk-ISM / HVC kinematics

Generated by `make_paper_figures.py` from the multi-ion Si-sequence absorbing-gas catalog
(`build_absorber_catalog.py`) + the v_ISM master table. Style: `m61_style.py`
(OVI_CGM_compare conventions — Computer-Modern usetex serif, inward ticks all sides,
framed legends, pdf+png). Output: `outputs/disk_ism_velocity/paper_figures/*.{pdf,png}`.

## Sample & ions
- **1,119,920** absorbing ray cells over the **14,400** sightlines (20 SIDs × 360 α × 2 modes).
- Six gas ions available in the combined ray, spanning the ionization/temperature sequence:
  **H I → C II → Si II → Si III → Si IV → N V** (neutral/cool ~10^4 K → warm-hot ~10^5.3 K).
- Every distribution is weighted by **each ion's own column**, so each figure is genuinely
  multi-phase. dv = v_los − v_ISM; class by |dv|: ISM <40, IVC 40–100, HVC >100.
- Full galaxy-frame 3D velocity per cell: v_r (galactocentric radial; <0 inflow, >0 outflow),
  v_z (perpendicular to disk), + metallicity and temperature.
- Not in the combined ray: O I (spectrum only), O VI / C IV / Mg II (would need ray regeneration).

## Headline science
- **HVCs are a multi-phase, ionization-stratified population.** Cool low-ion HVCs (H I, Si II) are
  sharply velocity-confined near the ISM and **inflow-dominated (~68%)** — accretion / fountain return.
  The warm-hot phase (Si IV, N V) is **kinematically broad, HVC-rich (34% of N V column), and balanced
  toward outflow** — the wind/ejecta signature, reinforced by its higher outflow metallicity.
- Including all ions was essential: Si II alone gave "HVCs are inflowing enriched fountain gas"; the
  full sequence reveals the co-existing warm-hot outflow that Si II is blind to.
