# Paper figures — multi-ion disk-ISM / HVC kinematics

Generated by `make_paper_figures.py` from the multi-ion absorbing-gas catalog
(`build_absorber_catalog.py`) + the v_ISM master table. Style: `m61_style.py`
(OVI_CGM_compare conventions — Computer-Modern usetex serif, inward ticks all sides,
framed legends, pdf+png). Output: `outputs/disk_ism_velocity/paper_figures/*.{pdf,png}`.

## Sample & ions
- **1.29M** absorbing ray cells over the **14,400** sightlines (20 SIDs × 360 α × 2 modes),
  read from the **combined `all_rays_L2Rvir.h5`** — the SAME ray product v_ISM was built on.
- Six ions are native to that ray; the other five (**O VI, C IV, O I, Mg II, Fe II**) are
  recomputed per ray from the stored density/T/metallicity/z with Trident's ionization tables.
- Figures use the ionization/temperature sequence **H I → C II → Si II → Si III → Si IV →
  C IV → N V → O VI** (⟨log T⟩ = 4.05 → 5.55; O I/Mg II/Fe II are in the catalog too).
- Each distribution is weighted by **each ion's own column** (multi-phase). dv = v_los − v_ISM;
  class by |dv|: ISM <40, IVC 40–100, HVC >100. Full galaxy-frame 3D velocity per cell:
  v_r (galactocentric radial; <0 inflow, >0 outflow), v_z (perp to disk); + Z and T.

## Validation
- The six native ions **reproduce the earlier single-ion (Si II) run exactly** (H I 12%, C II 7%,
  Si II 7%, Si III 10%, Si IV 22%, N V 34% HVC), confirming ray/v_ISM consistency; N_HI-weighted
  |dv|<40 = 0.73 (disk-dominated). O VI/C IV/etc. extend the sequence with correct peak temperatures.

## Headline science
**HVCs are an ionization-stratified, multi-phase population.** Cool low ions (H I, Si II) are
velocity-confined near the ISM and **~68% inflowing** (accretion / galactic-fountain return). The
warm-hot phase — C IV, N V, and especially **O VI (⟨log T⟩=5.5, 35% of its column in HVCs, 52%
outflow)** — is kinematically broad and outflow-dominated (the wind/ejecta signature, reinforced
by higher outflow metallicity). Restricting to Si II alone hides the co-existing warm-hot outflow.
